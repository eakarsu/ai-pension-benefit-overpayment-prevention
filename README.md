# Pension Benefit Overpayment Prevention

Prevent and resolve pension overpayments caused by eligibility, life-event, formula, offset, and beneficiary changes.

**Primary buyer:** Defined-benefit plans and public pension systems. **Evidence:** plan provisions, participant service, compensation, elections, beneficiaries, life status, payroll, COLAs, offsets, recoupments, and appeals.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Plan provision library
- Participant service history
- Compensation averaging
- Benefit formula calculation
- Election option validation
- Beneficiary entitlement
- Life-status monitoring
- COLA calculation
- Social Security offset
- Workers compensation offset
- Payroll payment reconciliation
- Overpayment detection
- Recoupment plan workflow
- Member appeal evidence
- Plan liability analytics
